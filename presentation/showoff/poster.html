Work Smarter, Not Harder

An AI training-readiness coach
TEAM Lior Ben Sidi Shiri Haboob Elad Nachalieli

What it does

  • Log sleep, fatigue, soreness and load each morning.
  • A locally-trained Random Forest calls your readiness.
  • Ready, Moderate, or Rest — with a confidence score.
  • It becomes an action plan: what to train, calories to eat.
  • A community forum and real-time DMs keep you accountable.

Under the hood

  • Three Docker containers; only the web tier is exposed.
  • Two-step email OTP login with signed session IDs.
  • Email verification and 2-step login codes over SMTP.
  • Custom domain on Azure; HTTPS certificates auto-renewed.
  • CI/CD auto-deploys green commits; all five test types.
